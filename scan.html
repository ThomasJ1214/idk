📷 Prepare for Your 3D Scan

  • 1 Distance: Stand 3–4 feet from your camera so your full upper body is visible.
  • 2 Lighting: Use bright, even light from the front. Avoid backlighting or harsh shadows.
  • 3 Clothing: Wear fitted, non-reflective clothes. Avoid pure black, pure white, or loose fabric.
  • 4 Background: Stand in front of a plain, uncluttered wall.
  • 5 Rotation: You'll turn slowly through 8 positions (every 45°). Keep your feet planted and rotate your upper body. Stay as still as possible during each capture.
