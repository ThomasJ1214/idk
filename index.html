How It Works

Everything runs in your browser

📷

Webcam Access

Select any connected camera. Your video stream stays on your device — nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere.

🤖

AI Landmark Detection

MediaPipe Hands detects 21 key points on each hand 30+ times per second, directly inside your browser using WebAssembly.

🦴

Skeleton Overlay

A live skeletal mesh is drawn over your camera feed, connecting all finger joints and palm landmarks in real time.

✋

Gesture Recognition

Recognizes five hand poses: Open Hand, Fist, Pointing, Peace sign, and Thumbs Up — updated every frame.

📍

Live Coordinates

See your wrist position as both a normalized ratio (0–1) and pixel coordinates, updated continuously.

🔒

100% Private

No server, no account, no tracking. All processing happens locally in your browser. Close the tab and it's gone.
